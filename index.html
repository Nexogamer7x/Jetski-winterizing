What's Included

Complete Jetski Winterizing Services

Every service is available a la carte, but most owners choose the full winterizing package for total peace of mind through the off-season.

🌫️

Engine Fogging

We fog the engine and internal components with a protective oil to prevent rust and corrosion on cylinder walls during storage.

❄️

Coolant Flush

Old coolant is flushed and replaced to protect against freezing temperatures and keep your cooling system running clean next season.

⛽

Fuel Stabilizer

We treat the fuel system with stabilizer to prevent gum, varnish, and ethanol damage from sitting fuel over the winter.

✨

Full Detail

A complete wash, hull polish, and interior wipe-down so your ski goes into storage clean and comes out looking sharp in spring.

💧

Water Drain

All water is drained from the exhaust, cooling passages, and bilge to prevent freeze damage and cracked components.

🛡️

Corrosion Protection

Metal fittings, fasteners, and exposed components are treated with corrosion inhibitor to fight rust through the off-season.

🔋

Battery Protection

We service or disconnect the battery and apply a maintainer setup so it's fully charged and ready to go when spring arrives.

Why Winterize?

East Tennessee winters bring hard freezes that can crack engine blocks, ruin fuel systems, and seize up components left unprotected. A proper winterizing service now saves you from costly repairs — or a ski that won't start — come spring.
